Hi my name is Evan. I’m the Chief Data & AI Officer at <a href = "https://www.wri.org/data" target = "_blank">World Resources Institute</a>. I build technical institutions and teams that help communities thrive and adapt to change. Views expressed here are fully my own.

Prior to joining WRI, I was Director of Innovation and Lead Data Scientist at the Rockefeller Foundation, where I founded its AI portfolio and served as its first data scientist. Before that I led research and analytics at Media Development Investment Fund, managed media and technology programs for IREX across the Middle East, and was a Fulbright Fellow in Azerbaijan. I'm a data scientist and cartographer by training with a master's in quantitative methods from Columbia University and bachelor's in political science and psychology from Hiram College.

On the side, I still make maps, mostly about American cities. Projects include <a href = "https://www.arcadiapublishing.com/products/9781948742559" target = "_blank">Cleveland in 50 Maps</a>, the inaugural title in Belt Publishing's 50 Maps Series and an atlas of my home region, Northeast Ohio; and the data visualization for <a href = "https://www.pbs.org/independentlens/documentaries/rat-film/" target = "_blank">Rat Film</a>, an award-winning documentary that uses Baltimore's rats to explore the city's past, present, and future.
